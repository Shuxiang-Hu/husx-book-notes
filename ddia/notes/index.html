The Big Ideas Behind Reliable, Scalable, and Maintainable Systems — Martin Kleppmann
12 Chapters 3 Parts O'Reilly, 2017

Part I — Foundations of Data Systems
Chapter 01
Reliable, Scalable & Maintainable Applications
The three non-functional goals every data system must satisfy, how to measure them, and trade-offs involved.
Chapter 02
Data Models & Query Languages
Relational vs document vs graph models; SQL, MapReduce, Cypher, SPARQL — when to choose each.
Chapter 03
Storage and Retrieval
How databases store data internally: hash indexes, SSTables, LSM-trees, B-trees, OLAP vs OLTP, column storage.
Chapter 04
Encoding and Evolution
Serialization formats (JSON, Thrift, Protobuf, Avro), backward/forward compatibility, modes of dataflow.
Part II — Distributed Data
Chapter 05
Replication
Leader-follower, multi-leader, leaderless replication; replication lag problems and solutions.
Chapter 06
Partitioning
Sharding strategies: key-range, hash, secondary indexes; rebalancing; request routing.
Chapter 07
Transactions
ACID, isolation levels, lost updates, write skew, phantoms; 2PL vs serializable snapshot isolation.
Chapter 08
Trouble with Distributed Systems
Unreliable networks, unreliable clocks, process pauses, fencing tokens, Byzantine faults.
Chapter 09
Consistency and Consensus
Linearizability, ordering guarantees, 2PC, fault-tolerant consensus (Raft/Paxos), ZooKeeper.
Part III — Derived Data
Chapter 10
Batch Processing
MapReduce, HDFS, join strategies, beyond MapReduce: Spark/Flink dataflow engines.
Chapter 11
Stream Processing
Event streams, Kafka, CDC, event sourcing, windowing, stream joins, exactly-once semantics.
Chapter 12
The Future of Data Systems
Data integration, Lambda vs Kappa, unbundling databases, end-to-end correctness, ethics.
