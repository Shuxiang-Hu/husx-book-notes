⚙️ Part II: Distributed Data

Chapters 5-9: Replication, Partitioning, Transactions, and Consensus

🧠 Part II Mind Map

📖 Overview

💡 Why Distributed?

Part II moves from data stored on one machine to data distributed across multiple machines. This is often necessary for scalability but brings unique challenges: network unreliability, clock skew, and the fundamental difficulty of achieving consensus.

📦

Chapter 5: Replication

Keeping data synchronized across multiple nodes. Leader-follower, multi-leader, and leaderless replication.

🔢

Chapter 6: Partitioning

Splitting data across shards. Key-range and hash partitioning. Rebalancing when nodes change.

📝

Chapter 7: Transactions

ACID properties and isolation levels. From read-committed to serializable.

⚠️

Chapter 8: Distributed Problems

Networks are unreliable, clocks are uncertain, and nodes fail.

🎯

Chapter 9: Consensus

Linearizability, total order broadcast, Paxos, Raft, and the CAP theorem.
