Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach

James F. Kurose & Keith W. Ross — 8th Edition  ·  Reading Notes

These notes follow the layered top-down structure of the textbook — from user-facing application protocols down through transport, network, and link layers, finishing with wireless networks and security. Each chapter page contains concept summaries, SVG architecture diagrams, and comparison tables.
